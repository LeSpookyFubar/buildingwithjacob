HOW TO READ IT

Signals, not scare tactics.

Cyber Pulse is designed to teach what different kinds of security activity mean while keeping uncertainty visible.

01 / OBSERVE

Something happened.

A DNS lookup, web event, authentication attempt, or other signal is simply an observation until context says otherwise.

02 / CORRELATE

Look for patterns.

Repeated or related events matter more than isolated dots. This mirrors Honeygotchi's memory and anomaly engines.

03 / EXPLAIN

Tell people why.

The goal is not a wall of red warnings. The goal is a clear explanation of what is known, uncertain, and worth attention.
