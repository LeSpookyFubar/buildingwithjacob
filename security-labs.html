Network & Security Labs

The less glamorous work that makes the bigger projects believable.

This is where I sharpen the systems knowledge underneath cybersecurity: Linux, networking, cloud operations, troubleshooting, identity, logging, and defensive monitoring.

LAB TRACKS

Skills that connect instead of living in silos.

The goal is to understand the machine, the network around it, and the evidence it leaves behind.

01

Linux & systems

Services, processes, permissions, packages, storage, logs, SSH, and system health.

02

Networking

Addressing, DNS, routing basics, packet flow, ARP, connectivity, and structured troubleshooting.

03

Cloud & identity

Cloud resource management, IAM concepts, least privilege, monitoring, automation, and resilience.

04

Detection

Logs, SIEM concepts, endpoint telemetry, event correlation, and useful alerts instead of noise.

05

Hardening

Reducing attack surface, patching, account controls, firewalling, and configuration review.

06

Troubleshooting

Hypothesis-driven diagnosis using tools like ping, tracert, nslookup, ARP, netstat, and system logs.

PORTFOLIO EVIDENCE

What belongs here as the labs grow.

Every lab should leave proof that another engineer can inspect.

01Network diagrams

Topology, trust boundaries, interfaces, and service relationships.

02Configuration notes

What changed, why it changed, and how it was verified.

03Logs & detections

Examples of expected events, suspicious events, and tuned alerts.

04Failure write-ups

Symptoms, hypotheses, tests, root cause, and the final fix.
